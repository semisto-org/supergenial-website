Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom project cost?

It depends on what it replaces. As a rough guide, a tool focused on a single process starts around €5,000, and a complete back-office replacing several subscriptions runs into tens of thousands of euros. The exact fixed-price quote comes out of the AI Assessment.

Why not bill by the hour?

Because with AI agents, the same feature can take ten times less time than before. Hourly billing would reward slowness. You pay for a result, at a price known upfront.

What is a “point”?

The unit for the small improvements included in the subscription, instead of hours. 1 point: a small tweak (a field, a label). 2 points: a small improvement (a filter, an automatic e-mail). About 5 points: a full screen. Beyond the quota, the change is priced separately, upfront.

Do unused points roll over?

The quota renews every month and does not accumulate. If your needs grow for good, we adjust the tier rather than stockpile.

Can I change tier or cancel?

Yes. The subscription has no lock-in and can be changed or cancelled from one month to the next. And the code stays yours in every case.
