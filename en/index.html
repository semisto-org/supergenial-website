What costs a lot without showing

The spreadsheets that run the company

Orders, registrations, stock, schedules: everything goes through a file everyone is afraid to open. It costs nothing to buy. You pay for it in hours of copying, in mistakes and in depending on a single person.

  • The same information typed two or three times
  • Versions that contradict each other
  • Only one person who knows how it works

The subscriptions that pile up

Forms, calendar, e-mailing, CRM, automations to connect it all. Each subscription is reasonable. Their sum no longer is, and it climbs with every new person on the team.

  • A per-user price that follows your growth
  • Tiers that jump at the worst moment
  • Full products paid for three features
