Good to know

Frequently asked questions

My application isn't built with Rails. Is it still possible?

Yes. Ruby on Rails is my long-standing speciality, but the approach is the same for any web application: PHP, Node, Python, Django, Laravel… The audit says exactly what is feasible and at what price.

My application has no automated tests. Is that a problem?

That is the case for most applications I take over. Before touching anything, I add tests on your critical paths: they act as a safety net throughout the upgrade, and they stay yours afterwards.

What can break during the upgrade?

Nothing in production: everything happens first on a test environment, version by version. The switch only happens once each step is validated, and we can roll back at any time.

The original developer is gone and there is no documentation.

A classic situation. I only need access to the code and the server: AI agents reconstruct how the application works by reading all of its code. I produce the documentation as an output.

Do I need to move my application to another host?

Not necessarily. Your server can stay where it is; if the current hosting is a problem, I can offer a migration to Infomaniak, in Switzerland. Either way, you keep control and ownership of your infrastructure.
