What the assessment looks at

Three questions, answers in numbers.

Where does your team's time go?Copying, checking, chasing, the tasks an AI agent could prepare for a human to approve.
What do your subscriptions really cost?A full inventory of your online software: per-user prices, tiers, features actually used, overlaps.
What is worth building?Custom versus subscriptions, compared over five years. Sometimes the right answer is to build nothing.

How it runs

Two weeks, five steps.

I come to you, listen to your teams describe their real working day, and leave with enough to map the whole company. Your time: about one hour per person interviewed, plus the presentation.

  1. ScopingYour priorities, constraints, current tools and subscriptions.
  2. InterviewsThe people who do the work, not just management.
  3. MappingYour processes, information flows and spreadsheets.
  4. AnalysisEach opportunity rated for impact (hours, euros) and feasibility.
  5. PresentationThe report presented and discussed with you, decisions in hand.

Frequently asked questions

Why pay, when free AI audits exist?

A free audit is a sales pitch in disguise: it always concludes you should buy what its author sells. Here you pay for real work, interviews, a process map, numbers, and the report is yours. You can implement it with me, with someone else, or on your own.

What if the assessment finds nothing worth doing?

Then the report will say so, with the reasons. There are almost always a few hours a week to win back or a subscription too many, but knowing you should not invest now saves you far more than the cost of an assessment.

Do we need to be “digital” already for this to make sense?

No. I start from your real processes, even if they live on paper, in notebooks or in two people's heads. That is exactly where the assessment uncovers the most value.

How much time does it take from my team?

About one hour per person interviewed, a kick-off meeting and a 90-minute presentation at the end. The rest of the work happens on my side.

Will you tell me to replace everything with custom software?

No. A custom tool has a monthly cost too. Standard needs (accounting, e-mail) are often better served by off-the-shelf software. The assessment compares both options over five years, honestly.

Is there public funding for this kind of work?

Walloon schemes supporting digital and AI projects (Start IA, chèques-entreprises) change regularly. Depending on your situation and the timing of calls, I point you to what exists when you get in touch.
