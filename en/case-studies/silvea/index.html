Silvéa — health and connection to the living world

The problem: publishing activities, taking paid registrations and running the site without depending on a developer.

WebsiteSilvéa ASBL · 2026 · Visit the siteRails 8 Inertia + React TypeScript Tailwind CSS Stripe

The context

Silvéa is a Walloon non-profit offering activities at the intersection of human health and nature connection — forest immersions, forest therapy, tailor-made programmes — for individuals, groups and healthcare professionals.

The need

The organisation needed a real website: presenting its offerings, publishing its activity calendar, taking registrations and payments online, running a resources blog, and managing all of it autonomously — without depending on a developer for every update.

The solution

A complete web application, built with AI agents from architecture to deployment: five content pages, a calendar with registration and Stripe payments (promo codes and refunds included), a blog, a GDPR-compliant newsletter, and four admin panels so the team manages activities, content and registrations themselves.

The result

A fast, accessible, SEO-minded website (structured data, semantic HTML), pixel-faithful to the designs, that the organisation keeps alive day to day without technical help.
